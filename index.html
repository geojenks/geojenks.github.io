George Jenkinson

Postdoctoral Researcher  ·  University of Bath

Exploring mathematically driven robotic mechanisms to better understand human ones.

My research spans tactile sensing, dexterous manipulation, swarm robotics, machine vision and learning with a particular focus on assistive technology. I completed my PhD in Bristol working across the Dexterous Manipulation and Soft Robotics research groups, joined the Centre for Print Research as a postdoc, and won funding for two small research projects focussing on extracting tactile properties from visual cues, and developing an assistive device to help people practice reading Braille independently. I am currently part of the ARIA Robot Dexterity programme at the University of Bath.

Alongside academia I explore nature, music, food, and any other pursuit I find enriching.
