Accessibility

My research has a particular focus on assistive technology, so I want this site to work for as many people as possible. This page says what it does to help, and where it still falls short.

Options on every page

Every page has a round button in the top right corner. It opens a panel with these options:

  • Text size: Default, Large or Larger. Large makes the text about a fifth bigger, Larger about a third. Your browser's own zoom still works as well.
  • Contrast: High gives black or white text on plain backgrounds, stronger lines and underlined links. Text on the picture tiles moves onto a solid dark panel.
  • Theme: System, Light or Dark. This is on the home page only, for now. System follows your device.
  • Motion: System or Reduce. Reduce stops the animations straight away: the moving wave diagram, the headshot re-stitching itself and the drifting threads in dark mode. System follows your device.
  • Readable font: switches to Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readers with low vision, with a little more spacing.
  • Plain view: hides the wave diagram and the stitching, and turns the picture tiles into plain text without photographs.
  • Reset all puts everything back to the defaults.

Your choices apply on every page. They are saved in this browser only, and are not sent to me or anyone else. The one exception is the readable font: when you turn it on, your browser downloads it from Google Fonts.

Your device's settings

  • Reduced motion: if your device asks for less motion, the animations stay still.
  • Dark mode: the home page follows your device's light or dark setting. The other pages are light only, for now.
  • Forced colours (such as Windows contrast themes): the site lets your colours take over, and hides the decoration.

Using a keyboard

  • The first thing you reach with Tab is a "Skip to content" link.
  • The controls are real buttons, links and sliders, so they work with Tab, Enter, Space and the arrow keys.
  • Escape closes the options panel and the pop-up windows.

The animations

The wave diagram and the embroidered headshot on the home page are extras. You don't need them to use the site. The headshot has a text label, and the diagram has a caption and labelled controls. Plain view hides both.

Known problems

  • Light and dark themes are on the home page only.
  • Some publication thumbnails are placeholders, and a few are missing.
  • Most videos on the Publications page are YouTube embeds. YouTube's player has its own captions and keyboard controls, which I don't control. The other videos, and the video and radio clips on the In the Media page, play from this site and have no captions or transcripts yet.
  • The video and audio players start playing as soon as they open. They, and the text pop-ups on the "…the Rest" page, close with Escape, but they don't yet move keyboard focus into themselves, and screen readers aren't told they are dialogs.
  • Text over photographs can be hard to read in places. The worst is the ARTEMIS tile on the Projects page, which has a light image. High contrast or Plain view fixes this.
  • Some small text is paler than it should be: the line with my email address on the home page, and the link back to the home page in the header of the other pages. High contrast fixes this.
  • Some links open in a new tab without saying so.

Standards

I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site has not been formally audited, so I don't claim that it meets them.

Reporting a problem

If something doesn't work for you, please tell me: gj525 (at) bath.ac.uk. It helps to know which page, what you were trying to do, and what you use to browse (for example a screen reader or zoom), if you're happy to say.
